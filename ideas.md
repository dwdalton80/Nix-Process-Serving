# Nix Process Serving — Design Directions

### Approach 1: The Oklahoma Docket
**Very Brief Intro:** A composed, legal-editorial landing page that pairs courthouse gravitas with warm, local confidence. It turns the service path into a clear sequence instead of an intimidating legal maze.

## Selected Approach: The Oklahoma Docket

### Design Movement
**Contemporary legal editorial** with the measured authority of a court docket and the practical warmth of an Oklahoma service business.

### Core Principles
1. **Directness over decoration:** Every section earns its place by clarifying a service, coverage, or next action.
2. **Field-ready authority:** Structured lines, case-file markers, and map cues suggest dependable movement through a legal process.
3. **Warm professionalism:** Courtroom seriousness is softened with natural ivory surfaces, burnished-gold accents, and approachable language.
4. **Local confidence:** Southern Oklahoma is treated as a specific service territory rather than generic geography.

### Color Philosophy
Deep **Nix Red** conveys decisive action and carries primary calls to action. A dark **Docket Black** grounds legal credibility, parchment white creates breathing room and paper-like tactility, while restrained **Brass Gold** signals care and formality. Red is concentrated at points where a visitor should take action; gold is a measured framing device rather than decoration.

### Layout Paradigm
The page follows a **case-file progression**: a strong asymmetric introduction, a numbered route through the core services, and a closing contact docket. At wider breakpoints, content sits on an offset editorial rail rather than a centered card grid. The result feels structured and directional, like documents moving through a reliable process.

### Signature Elements
1. A red “case tab” motif that labels services and sections.
2. Fine brass rules and docket-style reference numbers used as dividers and micro-details.
3. A South Oklahoma coverage mark with a simplified territorial contour and location pin.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions feel precise and intentional: service items brighten and lift minimally on hover, while contact actions press in with a short physical response. Navigation anchors are calm, fast, and clear.

### Animation
Use restrained 180–260ms transform-and-opacity transitions with a snappy ease-out. On load, the hero’s content and docket labels rise in a brief stagger; no looping decorative motion. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing entrance transforms and leaving content fully visible.

### Typography System
**DM Serif Display** handles the dignified, high-contrast display moments and section titles. **Manrope** supplies compact, highly legible body copy and labels. Headlines use a large editorial scale; labels use uppercase, tracked Manrope; essential call-to-action language is sentence case and compact.

### Brand Essence
**Nix Process Serving moves Southern Oklahoma legal documents with the speed, precision, and personal accountability local clients need.**

Personality: **dependable, exacting, local.**

### Brand Voice
Clear, businesslike, and reassuring. Headlines should name the outcome; CTAs should tell visitors exactly what to do next. Avoid vague claims and legalese.

Example: “Documents in motion. Confidence intact.”

Example: “Call now to place your service request.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the supplied Nix Process Serving mark as the authoritative brand asset. Its envelope-bearing server silhouette stays visible and is paired with a clean red-and-brass system; it is never reduced to a tiny, generic favicon-sized logo in navigation.
